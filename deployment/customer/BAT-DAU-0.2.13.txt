OUFEIDUN LOCAL 0.2.13 - TU TIM MAY CHU QUA WI-FI / LAN

MAY CHU DA CAI
1. Giai nen bo nay vao folder bat ky, ngoai folder du lieu dang chay.
2. Chay 01-MAY-CHU/CAP-NHAT.bat. Nhap folder du lieu CU neu chuong trinh chua tim thay.
3. Khong cai lai MySQL, khong tao DB hay tai khoan moi. Cap nhat backup truoc migration.
4. Chay KIEM-TRA-LOI.bat, sau do TAO-BO-MAY-NHAN-VIEN.bat.
5. Buoc 4 tao server-profile.json RIENG cua cua hang trong 02-MAY-CLIENT.

MAY NHAN VIEN (CA MAY CHU NEU CUNG BAN HANG)
1. Copy TOAN BO 02-MAY-CLIENT da tao tu may chu cua hang.
2. Dong app cu. Chay MO-APP.bat: app portable tu ket noi, khong can nhap IP,
   khong can cai MySQL/Node, khong can sua hosts hay quyen Admin.
3. Khong tach rieng Oufeidun.exe; giu ca folder App va server-profile.json.
4. Muon cap nhat shortcut app da cai: CAP-NHAT.bat. Cau hinh cu van duoc giu;
   neu co server-profile.json thi ban moi su dung nhan dien nay.
5. Neu can tim lai: nut Tim may chu trong Wi-Fi / LAN o man dang nhap.
6. Chua co server-profile.json: app giu cach ket noi cu; khong tu chon may chu la.

SAU KHI TAT MAY / MAT DIEN
Bat may chu: MySQL/API/HTTPS tu khoi dong cung Windows. Neu loi, CHAY-MAY-CHU.bat
hoac KHOI-DONG-LAI.bat, sau do KIEM-TRA-LOI.bat va gui file trong logs.
Windows+L van ket noi; Sleep/tat may thi khong.

DIEU KIEN MANG
Wi-Fi va mang day deu duoc. Hai may phai lien lac duoc tren mang noi bo.
Tu tim IP dung broadcast IPv4 UDP 47631; du lieu dung HTTPS TCP 443.
Client Isolation/Guest Wi-Fi/firewall chan thi khong tu ket noi duoc.
Tu tim khong tu tao dinh tuyen giua hai mang khac nhau. Cau hinh IP cu con
truy cap duoc van duoc thu truoc. Khong can nhap MAC tren may nhan vien.

DU LIEU / BAO MAT
Giu database, anh, tai khoan va CA cu. Khong copy config/server.env hay
discovery-identity.json (khoa rieng) sang may nhan vien.
Bo client chi chua public key va chung chi CA cong khai cua cua hang.
Khong tu gui lai giao dich ban/thu tien neu mat ket noi.
Neu migration loi, giu ban ma cu/backup va lien he ho tro.
Installer chua co chu ky so Authenticode thuong mai.

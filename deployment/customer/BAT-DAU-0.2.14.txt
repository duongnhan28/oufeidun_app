- Excel tu tao ma chua co va nhap kho cung mot giao dich. Ma moi: chua phan loai,

- Sua nhap kho Excel: nhan dung tieu de, thu tu cot, sheet du lieu,

  Khi cac dong da giao du, co nut xac nhan hoan tat phan ghi chu.

CAP NHAT MAY CHU TRUOC
1. Dong app tren cac may; tam ngung ban hang trong luc cap nhat.
2. Giai nen bo moi vao mot folder rieng, ngoai folder du lieu dang chay.
   Khong xoa folder du lieu cu, khong ghi de thu cong vao app dang chay.
3. Chay 01-MAY-CHU/CAP-NHAT.bat va chap nhan quyen Administrator.
   Neu duoc hoi, chon folder du lieu CU (folder co config/server.env va app).
4. Chuong trinh kiem tra DB, backup DB/anh/config truoc khi thay API,
   chay migration can thiet va khoi dong lai. Cho thong bao thanh cong.
5. Chay KIEM-TRA-LOI.bat. Neu bao loi thi dung, giu backup/log de ho tro;
   chua cap nhat may nhan vien va khong chay CLEAN-DATA-GIU-ACCOUNT.bat.
6. Chay TAO-BO-MAY-NHAN-VIEN.bat de tao public profile rieng cua cua hang
   trong 02-MAY-CLIENT cung cap voi bo nay.

CAP NHAT MAY NHAN VIEN (VA MAY CHU NEU CUNG BAN HANG)
1. Copy TOAN BO 02-MAY-CLIENT da xuat o buoc 6 sang moi may.
2. Dong app cu. Neu dung app portable, chay MO-APP.bat trong folder MOI;
   shortcut cu tro vao folder cu thi can doi sang MO-APP.bat moi.
3. Neu dung app da cai/shortcut Desktop, chay CAP-NHAT.bat de cai ban moi.
   Cau hinh ket noi trong AppData duoc giu lai.
4. Kiem tra dang nhap, kho, 1 don cu, anh va nhap kho Excel truoc khi ban.
   Dung mau MOI 9 cot tai tu app (.xlsx, <=100 ma, <=10 MB):
   Ma kinh / Loai kinh / Hang dien thoai / Hang kinh / Gia ban / Gia si mac dinh /
   Gia von co dinh / Ma-dong may dung chung (cach nhau bang dau phay).
   Ma moi tu tao, ma cu cap nhat loai/hang/dong may va CA BA GIA theo file.
   So luong la LUONG NHAP THEM (0..1.000.000), KHONG phai ton cuoi.
   Nhap 0 neu chi sua danh muc/gia; khong bo trong. Tu tao phieu va lich su kho.
   Moi lan nhap file MOI se CONG TIEP, khong nhap lai file da hoan tat.
   Mua hang khong co gia mua trong mau nay; gia von co dinh luu rieng.
   File 8 cot CU khong co So luong van chi cap nhat danh muc, khong cong ton.
   Kho kinh hien So luong ton ngay canh Ma kinh. Giu anh/trang thai/lich su.
   Nhap so luong bang Nhap mot ma hoac file CU 3 cot Ma kinh/So luong/Gia nhap.

KHONG CAN CAI LAI MYSQL / NHAP LAI DB, IP HAY MAT KHAU
- Cap nhat ca API may chu va app cac may. Chi thay EXE thi chua du ban sua
  trang thai don thieu va nhap Excel danh muc va so luong 9 cot. Ban nay van la 0.2.14, khong them
  bang/cot MySQL; phai chay CAP-NHAT.bat du may dang hien thi 0.2.14.
- Giu nguyen database, anh, tai khoan, config, CA va nhan dien may chu.
- Khong copy config/server.env hoac discovery-identity.json sang may con.
- Bo moi khong chua IP, mat khau, CA hay private key cua may phat trien.
- Ghi chu don cu da bi ban cu xoa khong the tu khoi phuc.

SAU KHI TAT MAY / MAT DIEN
Bat may chu: MySQL/API/HTTPS tu khoi dong cung Windows theo cau hinh cu.
Neu loi, chay CHAY-MAY-CHU.bat hoac KHOI-DONG-LAI.bat, roi KIEM-TRA-LOI.bat.
Windows+L van ket noi; Sleep/tat may chu thi may con khong ban duoc.

KET NOI LAN / WI-FI
Hai may phai lien lac duoc trong mang noi bo. Public profile cua cua hang
cho phep tu tim IP qua UDP 47631 co chu ky, du lieu qua HTTPS TCP 443.
Thu ket noi cu truoc; khong can nhap MAC, khong sua hosts o che do moi.
Wi-Fi Guest/Client Isolation/firewall chan thi can sua mang.
Man Ma dung chung van can Internet de doc API tra cuu website.

GIOI HAN
Installer chua co chu ky so Authenticode thuong mai.
Da kiem thu tren may phat trien; can kiem tra ket noi may that tai cua hang.

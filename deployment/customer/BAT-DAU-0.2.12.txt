OUFEIDUN LOCAL 0.2.12 - MAY DA CAI

MAY CHU
1. Giai nen ca folder vao vi tri bat ky. Khong de bo cap nhat trong thu muc du lieu app dang chay.
2. Mo 01-MAY-CHU/MO-MENU.bat, chon Cap nhat phien ban.
3. Chuong trinh tim cau hinh cu tu server-info.json/tac vu Windows. Neu chua thay, nhap thu muc du lieu CU co config/server.env.
4. Khong nhap lai root MySQL; khong tao database moi; khong chay Cai lan dau tren may da cai.
5. Sau cap nhat, chon Kiem tra loi. MySQL, API, HTTPS phai OK.
6. Neu may chu cung ban hang, cap nhat app nhu may nhan vien.

MAY NHAN VIEN DA CAI
1. Copy 02-MAY-CLIENT, chay CAP-NHAT.bat. Giu nguyen ket noi dang dung.
2. Neu IP/hostname thay doi: may chu chay TAO-BO-MAY-NHAN-VIEN.bat; copy 02-MAY-CLIENT moi va chay SUA-KET-NOI.bat.
3. Khong can cai MySQL/Node tren may nhan vien.

SAU KHI TAT MAY/MAT DIEN
Bat may chu: cac tac vu API/HTTPS va MySQL duoc cau hinh khoi dong cung Windows.
Neu chua ban duoc: CHAY-MAY-CHU.bat; neu van loi: KIEM-TRA-LOI.bat va gui file trong logs.
Windows+L van cho ket noi; Sleep/tat may thi khong.

SUA CAU HINH
SUA-CAU-HINH.bat: Enter giu gia tri cu. Thu ket noi DB truoc khi luu. Giu hostname/chung chi cu.
Doi IP thi can IP da gan tren may chu va mang nhan vien truy cap duoc.
Script khong tu tao dinh tuyen giua hai mang, khong sua router.

DU LIEU
Database, anh, tai khoan, chung chi duoc giu. Backup DB/anh truoc cap nhat.
Neu migration that bai, giu ban ma cu va backup; lien he ho tro, khong tu chay go/cai lai.
Bo cai chua co chu ky so Authenticode thuong mai.

OUFEIDUN - CAP NHAT SERVER 0.1.4 CHO APP 0.1.4

Chi chay tren MAY CHU, khong chay tren may nhan vien.
Day la goi server; nut Cap nhat ung dung tren desktop chi cap nhat app.

1. Dong app tren ca may chu va may nhan vien trong luc cap nhat.
2. Giai nen ZIP ra folder MOI, nam NGOAI OufeidunServerData.
   Khong chep app trong ZIP de len folder du lieu bang tay.
3. Chay CAP-NHAT.bat, chap nhan quyen Administrator.
   Neu hoi folder du lieu CU: chon folder co config\server.env.
   Nhap DUONG DAN THUC TE da cai tren may chu dang cap nhat.
4. Cho thong bao Cap nhat thanh cong. Chuong trinh backup SQL/anh/config,
   giu ma server cu trong app-before-update-..., cap nhat API/migration
   con thieu va khoi dong dich vu. Khong cai lai MySQL, khong reset data.
   Neu server cu chua cap nhat tu thang 10, co the chay migrations 008-013:
   bo sung cau truc; giu tai khoan, don hang, ton kho va cau hinh hien co.
   Neu bao loi: dung lai va gui thong bao; khong xoa ban backup/app cu.
5. Chay KIEM-TRA-TON-DU-KIEN.bat. Phai hien server 0.1.4 va
   OK: API dang chay da ho tro ton du kien tu tat ca don nhap.
6. Mo lai app 0.1.4. Dang nhap lai neu duoc yeu cau; bam Tai lai du lieu.
   Tren the san pham phai co dong Trong nhap / Du kien.

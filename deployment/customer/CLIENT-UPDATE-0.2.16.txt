CAP NHAT MAY DA CAI APP
1. Giai nen toan bo ZIP vao thu muc moi.
2. Dong app Oufeidun, chay CAP-NHAT.bat.
3. Mo app bang shortcut cu, kiem tra version 0.2.16.
4. Mo Ma dung chung, tim iPhone 15 / Samsung A12, bam xem anh.
Cap nhat tung may dang chay app, ke ca may chu neu may chu cung ban hang.

CHAY PORTABLE
Mo MO-APP.bat trong bo moi; khong chi copy rieng Oufeidun.exe.
Giu nguyen cau hinh ket noi trong AppData/Oufeidun.
Neu bo portable cu co server-profile.json cua cua hang, copy file nay
vao cung thu muc voi MO-APP.bat trong bo moi. Khong dung profile cua
cua hang khac. May moi chua co cau hinh can bo client xuat tu may chu
cu (public profile/chung chi); bo cap nhat nay khong tu cai may chu.

KHONG CAN CAP NHAT MAY CHU/API HOAC DATABASE
Giu Local API 0.2.15, MySQL, tai khoan, kho, don hang, anh va backup.
Khong chay lai cai MySQL, migration, clean-data hoac xoa AppData.
Tra cuu Ma dung chung can Internet; ban hang van dung may chu LAN cu.
Khong tat xac minh SSL/TLS de khac phuc loi mang.

Bo 0.2.15 cu duoc giu nguyen. Neu can quay lai, dong app va cai lai
installer cu; khong khoi phuc/xoa database cho lan doi URL nay.
Installer chua ky Authenticode thuong mai. Chua nghiem thu cap nhat
tren hai may vat ly/router thuc te cua khach.

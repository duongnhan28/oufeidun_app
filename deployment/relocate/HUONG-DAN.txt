GOM THU MUC OUFEIDUN LOCAL - BO SUNG CHO 0.2.14

1. Copy/giai nen TOAN BO bo nay vao folder rieng tren MAY CHU.
2. Dong app tren tat ca may, tam ngung ban hang.
3. Chay GOM-THU-MUC.bat, chap nhan Administrator.
4. Mac dinh neu Windows o C: thi nguon la:
   C:\OufeidunServerData va C:\Oufeidun-Local-0.2.14
   Dich la C:\Oufeidun. Co the nhap duong dan khac, co khoang trang.
5. Xem lai 3 duong dan, nhap GOM THU MUC de xac nhan.
6. Cho THÀNH CÔNG. Thu dang nhap, kho, anh, don hang, PNG/Excel tren may con.

KET QUA
<folder moi>\OufeidunServerData
<folder moi>\Oufeidun-Local-0.2.14
<folder moi>\.relocation (XML task cu, trang thai; chi Admin/SYSTEM doc)

Cong cu dung API/HTTPS/backup, backup DB/anh/config, copy kiem tra checksum,
sua env/launcher/Caddy/server-info va cac duong dan Scheduled Tasks.
Giu nguyen trigger/settings/SYSTEM, IP, hostname, CA/public key va MySQL DB.
Kiem tra API/HTTPS, anh local neu co va tao backup thu trong folder moi.
Khong cai lai MySQL; khong di chuyen datadir cua MySQL; khong doi hosts/firewall.
Khong tu tao tai khoan, xoa du lieu, chay migration hay nang cap app.
API dang chay va bo ban giao phai cung version; cap nhat xong roi moi gom.

SHORTCUT / MAY NHAN VIEN
- App da cai: giu shortcut va cau hinh ket noi cu.
- App portable tren may chu: doi shortcut sang 02-MAY-CLIENT\MO-APP.bat
  trong folder moi. Neu mo shortcut cu thi van mo ban portable trong folder cu.
- May con van dung IP/hostname/CA cu; khong can copy DB/config rieng sang may con.

GIU BAN CU
Hai folder nguon khong bi xoa. Neu chuyen that bai, cong cu thu dung ban moi,
giu anh moi (neu co), tra task cu va chay lai ban cu. MySQL van la cung database,
khong restore DB tu backup va khong lam mat don da ghi.
Folder dich da ton tai thi cong cu dung, khong ghi de. Neu lan truoc loi, giu
ca hai ban va .relocation de ho tro; khong xoa folder dich hay chay lai tuy tien.
Neu mat dien/dong cua so giua luc chuyen, chay KHOI-PHUC-NEU-BI-NGAT.bat
tren may chu, nhap folder goc moi va KHOI PHUC. File nay chi dung cho lan
chuyen chua hoan tat; tu choi quay lai sau khi da bao thanh cong.
Chi xoa hai folder nguon thu cong sau khi may chu/may con va backup chay on,
shortcut da doi. Khong xoa folder moi hoac thu muc du lieu MySQL.

GIOI HAN KIEM THU
Co kiem thu co lap voi task/DB gia lap va file copy that. Khong tu di chuyen
du lieu/dich vu tren may phat trien; can kiem tra may nhan vien that tai cua hang.
